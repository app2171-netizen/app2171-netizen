**[🪵 Reinforcement Learning for Robotic Timber Assembly](https://github.com/app2171-netizen/rl-two-arm-timber-assembly)**

Two XArm7 robot arms learn to seat a timber joint to a **2 mm tolerance** in MuJoCo/robosuite. I designed four reward-shaping iterations and trained a **Soft Actor-Critic** policy that reached a stable evaluation reward of **1128 ± 3** and cut notch misalignment by **67%**. I also diagnosed the reward-hacking behavior that kept it from full success.

`Python` `Stable-Baselines3` `MuJoCo` `robosuite` `Gymnasium`
